Common Questions from Nigerian Clients

Can I pay in Naira? What payment methods do you accept?
Yes, absolutely. I accept payment in Naira via bank transfer, Flutterwave, or Paystack. I also accept USD via Wise, PayPal, or direct bank transfer if you prefer. The project price is fixed in your chosen currency, no exchange rate surprises.
Do you sign NDAs and respect data privacy?
Yes. I'm happy to sign your NDA, and I always handle client data in compliance with Nigeria's NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation). Your data stays confidential, is used only for your project, and is deleted or returned upon completion unless you request otherwise.
What if my data is messy or in Excel files?
That's actually the norm, not the exception, especially in Nigerian businesses. I specialize in cleaning and structuring messy data from Excel, Google Sheets, PDFs, WhatsApp exports, and disparate systems. Part of every project includes data cleaning, so you don't need to have "perfect" data to start.
Can you work with my existing tools (Paystack, Flutterwave, Shopify, etc.)?
Yes. I have experience integrating with the most common tools used by Nigerian businesses: Paystack, Flutterwave, Monnify, Shopify, WooCommerce, Google Analytics, Meta Ads, and more. If you're using something else, just ask, I can likely connect to it via API or export.
How quickly can we start?
After our free discovery call, I can usually send a scope and quote within 24 hours. Once you approve and make the initial payment, I can start within 2-3 business days. Most Starter Audits are delivered within 5 business days; Core Projects take 2-4 weeks depending on scope.
