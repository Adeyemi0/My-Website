What I Deliver for Retention

Every model is built around your specific business context — not a generic algorithm.

Churn Risk Scoring Model

A machine learning model that assigns each customer a churn probability score (0-100%) updated weekly — so you know exactly who to prioritize.

  • Features: purchase frequency, recency, support tickets, engagement metrics
  • Output: risk score + top 3 drivers per customer
  • Integration: CSV export, API endpoint, or direct dashboard connection
  • Accuracy: Typically 75-90% precision on at-risk identification

Segmented Retention Playbooks

Not all at-risk customers are the same. I help you design targeted interventions for each segment — win-back offers, product education, or support outreach.

  • High-value at-risk: personalized account manager outreach
  • Price-sensitive: targeted discount or bundle offers
  • Product-fit issues: onboarding reinforcement or feature tutorials
  • Automated triggers: email/SMS workflows based on risk score thresholds

Retention Dashboard (Power BI / Streamlit)

A live dashboard showing churn trends, at-risk customer lists, and intervention ROI — so your team can act on the model without needing data science skills.

  • Weekly churn rate by cohort and segment
  • Top at-risk customers with recommended actions
  • Intervention performance: which offers actually work
  • Revenue recovered vs. cost of retention campaigns

Production-Ready Code & Documentation

You own the model. I deliver clean, documented Python code with instructions for retraining, monitoring, and updating — no vendor lock-in.

  • Modular scikit-learn / XGBoost pipeline
  • Feature engineering scripts with data dictionary
  • Model monitoring: drift detection and retraining triggers
  • Handover session: your team can maintain it independently

Common Questions About Churn Prediction

How much historical data do I need?
Ideally 6-12 months of customer transaction and engagement data. However, I can often build a useful MVP with as little as 3 months if your data is clean and well-structured. We'll assess your data during the discovery call.
What if my definition of "churn" is complex?
That's actually ideal. Churn isn't always a binary cancel/no-cancel. I work with you to define meaningful churn events: 30 days inactive, subscription downgrade, reduced order frequency, etc. The model learns your business logic.
Can you integrate the model with our existing tools?
Yes. I can deliver the model as: a Python package you run on your schedule, a REST API endpoint, a scheduled CSV export to your data warehouse, or directly embedded in a Power BI/Streamlit dashboard. We choose what fits your workflow.
How do you measure model success?
Beyond technical metrics (precision, recall), we track business outcomes: % of at-risk customers successfully retained, revenue recovered vs. intervention cost, and reduction in overall churn rate. I help you set up tracking for these during deployment.
What does a churn prediction project cost?
Most projects range from $3,000-$8,000 depending on data complexity, model sophistication, and integration requirements. Simple scoring models start at $3K; end-to-end solutions with dashboard and automation are $6K-$8K. Fixed-price quote after discovery — no hourly surprises.
