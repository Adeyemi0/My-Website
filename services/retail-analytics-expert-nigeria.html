Common Questions from Retail Owners

What POS or inventory systems do you support?
I work with most popular systems in Nigeria: Kippa, Square, Loyverse, Vend, Lightspeed, Odoo, Sage, QuickBooks, and even custom Excel/CSV setups. If your system exports data (even as PDF or CSV), I can clean and unify it into a working analytics pipeline.
Will this work with poor internet or power supply?
Yes. I design dashboards with offline-first or low-bandwidth optimization in mind. Data can be synced in batches, exported to PDF/Excel for store managers, and hosted on lightweight platforms that don't require constant high-speed internet.
How long until we see results?
Most clients see quick wins (dead stock identification, reorder optimization, branch performance visibility) within 10-14 days of deployment. Forecasting models improve in accuracy over 30-60 days as they learn your sales patterns.
Do we need to hire a data person to maintain it?
No. I build systems designed for store managers and owners, not data scientists. You get automated refreshes, clear visual labels, and a 1-hour training session. I handle the heavy lifting, and optional retainer support keeps everything running smoothly.
How do you measure ROI for retail analytics?
We track: reduction in stockout days, decrease in dead inventory value, improvement in gross margin per category, and time saved on manual reporting. Most retailers recover the project cost within 1-2 months through better ordering and pricing visibility.
