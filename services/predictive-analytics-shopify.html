How We Build Your Predictive Engine

A lightweight, app-integrated process that doesn't disrupt your store operations.

1

Discovery & Data Mapping

We review your Shopify setup, connected apps (Klaviyo, Recharge, GA4, 3PL), and business goals. I map your data sources and identify predictive opportunities.

2

Data Pipeline & Cleaning

I connect to the Shopify Admin API, export historical orders, customer behavior, and marketing spend. Data is cleaned, deduplicated, and structured for modeling.

3

Model Training & Validation

I train predictive algorithms (LTV regression, churn classification, demand forecasting) and validate against holdout periods. We choose the model that balances accuracy with business actionability.

4

Integration & Dashboard Deployment

Predictions are synced to your marketing tools (Klaviyo segments, CRM tags) and visualized in a live Power BI/Looker dashboard. Automation handles weekly updates.

5

Handover & Optimization

You receive the model, documentation, and a training session. We run a 30-day optimization window to refine thresholds, adjust forecasts, and ensure full team adoption.

Common Questions from Shopify Founders

Do you need full Shopify admin access?
Yes, but only read-only API access or export permissions. I don't need to modify your store, apps, or theme. All data extraction is done securely via Shopify Admin API or CSV exports, and your data is never shared or stored long-term.
Will this replace my current Shopify apps (Triple Whale, Elevar, etc.)?
No. I build on top of your existing stack. Predictive outputs are synced back to your apps (e.g., Klaviyo segments, dashboard tools). Think of it as a "brain upgrade" for your current data ecosystem, not an app replacement.
How long until we see predictive insights?
Most brands see their first predictive models deployed within 14-21 days. Initial LTV/Churn scores and demand forecasts are available immediately. Accuracy improves over the next 30-45 days as the models learn your brand's specific patterns.
What data minimums do I need to start?
Ideally 6-12 months of order history with at least 1,000+ customers. If you're newer (<$50K/mo), we can start with foundational tracking and cohort modeling, then layer in predictive features as your data grows.
How do you measure ROI on predictive analytics?
We track: (1) Reduction in CAC via smarter ad allocation, (2) Increase in repeat purchase rate/LTV via targeted retention, (3) Decrease in stockout/dead inventory costs, and (4) Time saved on manual reporting. Most brands see 3-5x ROI within 60 days.
